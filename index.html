    Why restart it now

    The summit has some of the best material in application security: years of working sessions, hundreds of recorded talks and the people who gave them. It never fulfilled its potential as a place you could come back to, because nobody had the time to follow up, connect the sessions to the people, or give each participant their own path through it. The website that held it all got so complex that every new summit added exceptions and none of them was ever refactored away, and in the end it stalled.

    What has changed is that the follow-up can now be done. An agent can read the recordings and the pages, map who said what, build a page for every participant, and send the next steps after a session. That is what this version is for. Read the full story.

    A home for open projects

    • Proposed · first hosted project

      Agent Behaviour Policies

      A written description, for one agent in one deployment, of everything it can reach, what it was authorised to do, the gap between the two, and what stands in the way. Originated at RiskMandate, already open under CC BY 4.0 and Apache 2.0. RiskMandate stays a founding contributor and a commercial implementer, on the same terms as anyone else.
