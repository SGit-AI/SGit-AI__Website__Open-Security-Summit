## The formats {#formats}

### In person, in London {#london}

The in-person summits were the best of what the Open Security Summit did: a room of people who came to work, not to listen. The London events start smaller than the five-day weeks of 2017 to 2019, and keep the same rule. Every session is a working session, and every participant is expected to contribute.

### Remote working sessions {#remote}

From 2020 the summit ran online, first as a virtual week and then as monthly mini-summits. The remote sessions keep what worked there, the reach and the recordings, and add what was missing: the outcome written down, and the follow-up sent to the people in the session.

### Sessions for hosted projects {#project-sessions}

The [hosted projects](/projects/) will use the summit's sessions to work in the open: reviews of drafts, decisions that need more than one company in the room, and onboarding of new contributors.

## Get told first {#notify}

Until there is a list, watch [this site's repository](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Open-Security-Summit) or follow [@opensecsummit](https://twitter.com/opensecsummit). To propose a session, see [participate](/participate/#how).
