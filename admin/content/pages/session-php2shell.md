## The session {#session}

- **Format.** Online, one to two hours, recorded unless the participants say otherwise.
- **When.** Called when the people who need to be in it can make it. No date yet.
- **Owner.** Dinis Cruz.
- **Outcome.** A written summary of what the room knows that the sources do not, the open questions it closed or narrowed, and the next steps, sent to everyone who took part.

## The questions {#questions}

1. **The disclosure.** What broke on 20 October, and what a coordinated disclosure for a runtime this widely used should look like.
2. **AI-assisted discovery.** What the models did and what the people did, and what that means for the next engine bug.
3. **Offline or not.** Why the NSA, CISA and the UK NCSC said take systems offline if you cannot patch, while others said isolate or said nothing, and what a CISO should do with conflicting advice.
4. **The long tail.** PHP 8.1 and older are out of support, with source backports to 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1 that someone has to build. Who holds those servers, and what happens to them.
5. **Evidence over claims.** Attempts in the millions, no named compromise. How to say what happened without saying more than the evidence does.
