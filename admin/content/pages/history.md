## What stayed the same {#constant}

From 2008 to 2024 the format changed (villas, then Zoom) and the rule did not. In the words of every year's README: "This is not a conference with uni-directional presentations, this is a working summit with working sessions." The schedule was "led by you": anyone could create a session and add it. Sessions had organisers, not speakers, and everyone was a participant. Sponsors paid for the week, never for the content.

## Where these facts come from {#sources}

The repositories of the summit's websites, read on 21 October 2026: [oss2018](https://github.com/OpenSecuritySummit/oss2018), [oss2019](https://github.com/OpenSecuritySummit/oss2019), [oss2020](https://github.com/OpenSecuritySummit/oss2020) and [oss2021](https://github.com/OpenSecuritySummit/oss2021), the last of which is open-security-summit.org. Session and participant counts are pages in those repositories, which include some placeholders and cancelled sessions, so they are approximate. The 2008, 2011 and 2017 summits are from [the history of the OWASP in-person summits](https://diniscruz.ai/2025/06/07/history-and-analysis-of-owasp-in-person-summits.html) on diniscruz.ai. The 2020 attendance figures are the ones on open-security-summit.org's home page.
