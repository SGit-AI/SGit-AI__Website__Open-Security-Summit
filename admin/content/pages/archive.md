## The whole archive {#all}

Everything is still online, at its original address:

- [open-security-summit.org](https://open-security-summit.org/): the mini-summits, October 2020 to December 2024, with their schedules, session pages and recordings.
- [2020.open-security-summit.org](https://2020.open-security-summit.org/), [2019.open-security-summit.org](https://2019.open-security-summit.org/) and [2018.open-security-summit.org](https://2018.open-security-summit.org/): the summit weeks.
- [The summit's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCyse9b_2JLJUxKMLgSpOL-Q).

## What happens next to it {#next}

The next step is to read every session page and recording into records: who ran it, who took part, what was said, what it produced, and what it connects to. The old sites link people to sessions by matching names as text, with no identifiers, and the same track is spelt nearly forty ways, so this is a cleaning job as much as a reading one. Each person in it will get a page here, which they can correct. See [participate](/participate/#new).
